Frequently Asked Question

Everything you need to know befor you register.

Is there a virtual attendance option?

Yes, all Pro and Team ticket holders get access to the live streamed main stage sessions plus 30 days of on-demand recordings. Standard tickets are in-person only.

Whats includeed in my ticket price?

Full refunds are available up to 30 days before the event. Within 30 days, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague at no extra charge instead.

Will sessions be recorded?

Most breakout sessions are recorded and made available to Pro and Team attendees. A handful of hands-on workshops are excluded due to their interactive format.

Is there a hotel or travel discount?

We've partnered with three hotels near the venue offering a discounted conference rate. The booking link and code will be emailed after you register.
